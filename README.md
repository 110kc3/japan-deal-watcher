## 🛡️ Formułki Inspekcji Zdjęciowej ZenMarket (500 JPY za 3 zdjęcia)

- **Dla PS Vita / PSP:**
  > *"Please turn on the console and take 1 clear photo of the screen displaying the main menu (to check for dead pixels or screen yellowing). Please take 1 photo of the open battery compartment, and 1 photo of the back serial label."*
- **Dla obiektywów analogowych:**
  > *"Please shine a flashlight through the lens elements and take 1 photo of the glass clarity (checking for fungus or haze), 1 photo of the front element, and 1 photo of the rear mount."*
- **Dla konsol stacjonarnych (Saturn, Dreamcast, PS2):**
  > *"Please take 1 photo of the overall front and top shell, 1 photo of the bottom label with serial number, and 1 photo of the controller ports."*
